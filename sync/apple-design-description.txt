Check a named Apple-native control against Apple's Human Interface Guidelines. Use only when an app design or review includes one of these: Apple Pay button, mark, or checkout; Sign in with Apple; system permission prompt; notification; Widget; Live Activity; Dynamic Island; system alert; action sheet; share sheet; system picker; SF Symbols on a system control; iPhone Duo system bar, fold, or outer display; Liquid Glass on system chrome; App Review rejection for design or branding. 僅在 App 設計或評審包含上述 Apple 原生功能時讀取，並只核對該功能。Do not use for general UI review, custom screens, design-system components, promo, search, PDP, cart, density, copy, or branded layout. 沒有上述功能就不要讀。
